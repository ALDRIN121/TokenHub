# Code of conduct

TokenHub welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help keep issues, pull requests, and other project spaces useful and respectful.

- Discuss ideas and code constructively. Critique the work without personal attacks.
- Be considerate when asking for help or reviewing someone else's contribution.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or real
  provider session data without their permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or spam other participants.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to enforce these guidelines. Repeated or serious violations may result
in exclusion from project spaces.

Use GitHub's [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
to report abusive content or conduct. Report software vulnerabilities privately
through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
